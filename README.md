# Fun-Math
Point and click office worker game with 8bit art style. 
You work as an office worker who has to complete his assignment by a deadline. 
While doing your assignment, you must deal with constant distractions that only serve to rage bait you. 
If your rage bait meter reaches a certain point, you lose your cool and do something that we haven't decided yet. 
You must deal with the distractions quickly to return to your tasks and complete it before its time to clock out.
